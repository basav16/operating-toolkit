How it runs

Runs on one Google Sheet and a short Apps Script: Slack digest at 8 AM, welcome emails as Gmail drafts, Week 1 on the calendar. See How it works. At ~20+ people, Rippling takes over account creation and removal.

Connect your data

1. Get the templateSeven tabs (Hires, Checklist, Tasks, Owners, Access, Week1, Settings), the same columns as the Google Sheet. Opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
2. Try it with your fileChange a start date, add a hire, mark a task Done. Save as .xlsx and load it here. It stays in your browser.
3. Go liveThe Sheet becomes the database, a short Apps Script posts to Slack, Gmail and Calendar, and this page runs privately inside Google.
