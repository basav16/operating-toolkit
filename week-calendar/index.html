How I run the week

Monday, 9:00 AM. Here's my week.

Inbox fire Onboarding project Investor update CEO + closing loops Team Running in background (others working while I do something else) Deadline Bars under each day: how far the two Friday projects are by end of that day
Assumed: ~15 people in SF; Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub, Linear, Ramp, Rippling; I have Slack and Google admin, not GitHub or Linear. Names in brackets, like [name], are placeholders. The plan lives in one Google Sheet: see the How it works tab.

Connect your data

1. Get the templateSix tabs (Inbox, Blocks, Messages, Background, Deadlines, Days), the same columns as the Google Sheet. Opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
2. Try it with your fileMove a block, add a message, mark an inbox item At risk. Save as .xlsx and load it here. It stays in your browser.
3. Go liveThe Sheet becomes the database, a short Apps Script posts to Slack, Gmail and Calendar, and this page runs privately inside Google.
