Operating toolkit

Three working tools for running a company's week, onboarding new hires, and keeping investors up to date. Each runs on sample data and can connect to a shared Google Sheet.

Week planA Monday inbox of seven fires turned into a full week plan, with every email and Slack drafted for approval. Onboarding hubEight hires over three weeks, about 190 tasks across seven owners, so every hire is ready on Day 1. Investor dashboardThe live numbers behind a quarterly investor update: ARR, customers, cash, runway, goals vs. results.
